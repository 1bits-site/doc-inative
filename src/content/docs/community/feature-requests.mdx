title: Feature requests
description: Propose what doesn't exist yet — proposing is not promising.
---

Have an idea? Open a [feature request](https://github.com/1bits-site/doc-inative/issues/new?template=feature-request.yml)
telling us:

- The proposal (`Implement SETGT…`)
- The IBM i reference (how IBM i behaves / where it is documented)
- The use case (what real program needs this?)

The community proposes. The roadmap stays curated.
