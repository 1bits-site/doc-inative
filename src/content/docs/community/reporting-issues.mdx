Since the runtime source is private, the community contributes knowledge:
bugs, cases, IBM i behavior, feature requests, documentation.

## How to report an IBM i difference

```text
IBM i version:
iNative version:

What were you trying to do?

IBM i behavior:

iNative behavior:

Expected:

Actual:

Minimal reproduction:

Logs:
```

`"No funciona"` can't be fixed. `"IBM i does X, iNative does Y, here is
a 10-line reproduction"` can — and becomes a [compatibility case](/community/ibmi-cases/).

## Pick the right form

- [🐛 Bug](https://github.com/1bits-site/doc-inative/issues/new?template=bug.yml) — iNative does something incorrectly.
- [🔄 IBM i Difference](https://github.com/1bits-site/doc-inative/issues/new?template=ibmi-difference.yml) — feed the compatibility base.
- [🚀 Feature Request](https://github.com/1bits-site/doc-inative/issues/new?template=feature-request.yml) — propose what doesn't exist.
- [🧪 Compatibility Case](https://github.com/1bits-site/doc-inative/issues/new?template=compatibility-case.yml) — a real IBM i program to mirror.
- [📖 Documentation](https://github.com/1bits-site/doc-inative/issues/new?template=documentation.yml) — errors or gaps in these docs.

Not sure yet? Start a [Discussion](/community/discussions/) instead.
