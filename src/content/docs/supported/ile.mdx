---
title: ILE
description: Modules, service programs, binder and activation groups.
---

- Modules (`CRTRPGMOD`, `CRTCPPMOD`, …) with procedures, exports and calling
  convention as catalog metadata.
- Binder: imports resolve against exports before any native build
  (`ILE0012` unresolved export, `ILE0013` invalid binding).
- Service programs (`CRTSRVPGM`) bundle modules under a signature.
- Activation groups (`*NEW`, `*CALLER`, named) are declared by the compiler
  and materialized by the runtime.

Background: [ILE concepts](/concepts/ile/) and [execution model](/concepts/execution-model/).
