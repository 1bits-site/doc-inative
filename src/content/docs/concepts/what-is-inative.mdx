---
title: What is iNative?
description: iNative is a local development environment that reproduces IBM i/ILE workflows.
---

iNative is an experimental **local development environment** designed to reproduce
IBM i/ILE development workflows locally: write RPGLE, compile, bind, run, and debug
— including 5250 screens — without an IBM i machine.

## What iNative is not

- An IBM i emulator
- A replacement for IBM i
- A production IBM i server
- A complete RPG or COBOL compiler
- 100% IBM i compatible

It is a tool for **learning, prototyping, and validating IBM i behavior locally**.
Compatibility is incremental and tracked in the [support matrix](/compatibility/matrix/).
