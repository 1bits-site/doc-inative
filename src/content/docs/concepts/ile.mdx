---
title: ILE
description: Modules, service programs, binding and activation groups in iNative.
---

iNative treats ILE as first-class objects, not compiler special cases:

```text
RPGLE ─┐
CLLE ──┤──→ modules ─→ binder ─→ program ─→ job
COBOL ─┤         ╲──→ service program ──╱
C ─────┘
```

- **Modules** (`CRTRPGMOD`, `CRTCPPMOD`, …) carry procedures, exports and
  calling convention as catalog metadata.
- **Binder** resolves imports against exports before any native build
  (`ILE0012` unresolved export, `ILE0013` invalid binding).
- **Service programs** (`CRTSRVPGM`) bundle modules with a signature.
- **Activation groups** are declared by the compiler (`*NEW`, `*CALLER`,
  named) and materialized by the runtime.

Adding a language means: frontend → shared IR → existing Runtime ABI.
