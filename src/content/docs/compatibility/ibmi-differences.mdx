## RPG

### Different behavior

- ** zoned numerics** surface as plain integers in some paths (display shows
  digits; packed arithmetic stays exact). Status: Open.

### Not implemented

- Printer files, commitment control, full BIF catalog. Status: Open.

## 5250

### Different behavior

- Colors are best-effort (the 5250 palette has 4 base colors); positions and
  fields are exact. Status: Accepted.
