---
title: Known limitations
description: Brutally honest list of what iNative cannot do yet. Updated every release.
---

## COBOL

Only initial infrastructure exists. Full COBOL support is not implemented.

## QUSRJOBI

Only the subset required by current runtime scenarios exists
(`JOBI0100`: job, user, number, program, library list).

## CL

Interpreter in development: `RTVJOBA`, `MONMSG`, core verbs. No full CL yet.

## C/C++

Native modules participate in binding via metadata; transpilation coverage
is experimental.

## IBM i compatibility

Not yet fully validated against IBM i. The [support matrix](/compatibility/matrix/)
is the contract; anything outside it should be assumed missing.
